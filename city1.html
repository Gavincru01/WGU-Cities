Baton Rouge

Louisiana’s Capital City — A Hub of Government and Southern Charm

About

  • Population: 220.907
  • Year Incorporated: 1817
  • Region: Capital Region of Louisiana
  • The average income level in Baton Rouge is slightly below the state average for Louisiana
    • Baton Rouge: approximately $46,000–$52,000
    • Louisiana state average income: $55,416
  • Baton Rouge is primarily considered urban with a significant suburban presence.

Baton Rouge is the state capital of Louisiana. It is the site of the only American Revolution battle fought outside the original 13 colonies. The city also boasts the USS Kidd, a World War II destroyer, and is home to Louisiana State university, known for its live tiger mascot, Mike the Tiger
