New Orleans

The Birthplace of Jazz, Voodoo Culture, and Mardi Gras

About

  • Population: 362,701
  • Year Incorporated: 1718 (By the French)
  • Region: Southeastern Region of Louisiana
  • The average income level in New Orleans is below the state average for Louisiana
    • New Orleans: ~$47,000
    • Louisiana state average income: $55,416
  • New Orleans is considered an Urban city.

New Orleans boasts a rich and unique history, filled with fascinating facts. It's known as the birthplace of jazz, a city with a vibrant Voodoo culture, and the site of the first documented opera performance in the United States.
