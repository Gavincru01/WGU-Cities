Lafayette

The Heart of Cajun Country and a Celebration of Southern Culture

About

  • Population: 122,280
  • Year Incorporated: 1836
  • Region: South-central part of the state, within the Acadiana Region
  • The average income level in Lafayette is slightly below the state average for Louisiana
    • Lafayette: ~$54,000
    • Louisiana state average income: $55,416
  • Lafayette is considered a blend of urban and suburban, with some rural characteristics.

Lafayette boasts a rich and diverse history, shaped by the convergence of various cultures. Initially known as Vermilionville, it was renamed after the Marquis de Lafayette, a French hero of the American Revolution. The city's development was influenced by indigenous populations, Acadian refugees, and subsequent waves of settlers from France, Spain, and other parts of the world. This mix of cultures resulted in the unique Cajun and Creole traditions that are celebrated today.
